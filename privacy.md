# Privacy Policy — YouTube Uploader

**Last updated: October 20, 2026**

## Overview

YouTube Uploader is a personal application used to upload videos to YouTube channels authorized by the developer.

## Information and access

The application uses Google OAuth 2.0 to authenticate Google accounts and requests permission to access relevant YouTube API functionality, including uploading videos and checking the authorized channel's identity.

The application may process video files and associated metadata, such as titles, descriptions, tags, and privacy settings, to perform uploads.

## How information is used

Information accessed through the application is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, identify the selected YouTube channel, and perform the requested upload operations.

## Storage and security

OAuth credentials and access tokens are intended to be stored locally by the developer. They should not be published in the public source repository or shared with unauthorized parties.

## Sharing

The application is not intended to sell personal information or share account credentials with third parties. YouTube processes uploaded videos and related data under Google's and YouTube's own terms and privacy policies.

## Data retention and revoking access

The developer can remove locally stored authorization tokens. Users can also review or revoke the application's Google account access through their Google Account security settings.
